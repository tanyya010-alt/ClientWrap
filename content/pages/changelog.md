# Changelog

New features and fixes, newest first. Lifetime (AppSumo) customers get every update to the features in their tier.

## 1.0.0 (September 2026): Launch
- **Client portal:** one private, branded, mobile-friendly link per client with results, invoices and next step.
- **Monthly wraps:** headline numbers, change vs. last month, AI or built-in summary, editable, PDF export, share link and scheduled delivery.
- **Data intake:** manual entry, CSV import with column mapping and error report, signed & idempotent webhook with Zapier, Make and n8n snippets.
- **Invoices:** PDF invoices, Stripe pay links on your own account, automatic status sync and receipts.
- **Reminders:** timezone-aware sequence (−3, +1, +7, +14, +30 days), quiet hours, per-client pause, unsubscribe, tone guardrails and full message log. WhatsApp/SMS via your Twilio (rolling out behind a feature flag).
- **Growth:** renewal suggestions, case studies with client approval, referral requests.
- **White-label (Agency):** remove branding, custom domain, custom email sender.
- **Onboarding:** 5-step guided setup, one-click demo workspace, templates for automation agencies, SEO, coaching and design.
- **Trust:** row-level security on every table, encrypted secrets, rate limiting, audit log, data export and account deletion.
