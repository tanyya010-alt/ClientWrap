# Public roadmap

What we're working on. Priorities are shaped by customer feedback; tell us what you need via [support](/help/support).

## Now
- WhatsApp & SMS reminders: general availability (currently behind a feature flag).
- More service templates (PPC/ads, bookkeeping, social media, web maintenance).

## Next
- Multi-currency summaries on the dashboard.
- Portal comments: let clients reply to a wrap from their results page.
- Google Sheets live sync (as a data source through the webhook).

## Later
- Additional languages for client-facing pages and emails.
- Report themes and custom sections.

## Not planned (by design)
Lead generation, CRM, project management, voice calls, native mobile apps, team roles and native analytics integrations: ClientWrap stays focused on wrapping up each client's month. Use the webhook to bring data from any tool.
