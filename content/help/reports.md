# Monthly reports ("wraps")

A wrap is a monthly report per client with **headline numbers**, **change vs. last month**, a **written summary of what changed**, and a **suggested next step**.

## Generate
Open a client → **Overview & reports** → pick a month → **Generate wrap**. Numbers are calculated from the results data for that calendar month (UTC), using each metric's Total/Average/Latest setting.

## Edit before sending
Everything is editable: the summary and the next step. If new data arrives, click **Refresh numbers**; your text is kept. **Rewrite summary** replaces the text with a fresh one.

### Who writes the summary?
- With your own [AI key](/help/ai-keys), the summary is written by the model you chose, using only your numbers.
- Without a key, ClientWrap's built-in writer produces a clear, factual summary from the data. Nothing is invented either way.

## Send, share, export
- **Send to client** emails the summary, attaches the PDF and links to the client's results page.
- **Send a test to me** sends the same email to you only.
- **Download PDF** anytime. Clients can also download PDFs from their results page.

## Scheduled delivery (Solo plan and up)
In **Client settings → Scheduled monthly wrap**, choose a day (1–28). On that day (after 8:00 in the client's timezone) ClientWrap generates the previous month's wrap and either sends it automatically, or emails you the draft to review, depending on the *Send automatically* box.
