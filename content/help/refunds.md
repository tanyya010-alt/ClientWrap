# Refunds & deactivation

Refunds for AppSumo purchases are handled by AppSumo under their refund policy. When a license is refunded, AppSumo deactivates it and ClientWrap applies the change automatically:

1. **Paid access stops immediately**: no new reports, invoices, reminders or scheduled sends; client portals show "temporarily unavailable".
2. **Your data stays read-only for 30 days.** You can log in, view everything and **export** it (Settings → Data & privacy → Export JSON or CSV).
3. **After 30 days** client data is permanently deleted. Your login stays, on the Free plan.

## Reactivating
Redeem a new or re-activated license (or choose a paid plan) within the 30 days and everything is restored exactly as it was.

## 24-hour cooldown
After a refund, a new license can be redeemed on the same account after 24 hours.
