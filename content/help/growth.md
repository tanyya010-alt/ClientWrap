# Growth tools (Growth plan)

Open **Growth** and choose a client.

## Renewal suggestion
**Generate** creates a suggestion from the client's latest results and contract end date (set it in Client settings). Edit it, tick **Show on the client's results page**, and it appears as "Looking ahead" on their portal.

## Case studies with client approval
**Draft from results** writes a short case study using only real numbers. Nothing can be published until the client approves:

1. Click **Ask client to approve**: they get an email with a link to review the draft.
2. They **approve** (typing their name) or **request changes** (you see their feedback).
3. Once approved, **Publish** creates a public page you can link to. Editing the text afterwards resets it to draft and requires a new approval.

## Referral requests
**Draft a referral request** writes a short, no-pressure email mentioning one real result. Edit and send. To avoid pestering, ClientWrap warns you if you asked the same client in the last 30 days.
