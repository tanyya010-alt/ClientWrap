# Clients & the client portal

Every client you add gets a **client workspace**: their metrics, reports, invoices, messages and consent records. Your plan decides how many active client workspaces you can have (archived and demo clients don't count).

## The results page (portal)
Each client has one private link, e.g. `https://app.clientwrap.app/p/…`. It needs no login and works well on phones. It shows:

- the latest sent monthly wrap (headline numbers, change vs. last month, summary and next step), with earlier months one tap away;
- open invoices with a **Pay now** button, plus paid invoices with PDFs;
- your renewal suggestion ("Looking ahead"), if you chose to show one;
- a case-study approval request, if you sent one.

The link is signed, so it can't be guessed. If it's ever shared by mistake, open the client and click **Reset link**: the old link stops working immediately.

## Branding
Your workspace color and logo are used everywhere. Each client can also have its own accent color and logo (Client settings). On the Agency plan you can remove ClientWrap branding and use your own domain; see [White-label](/help/white-label).

## Metrics in your client's own units
Metrics come from your service template and can be renamed, added or hidden in **Client settings → Metrics**. For each metric choose:

- **Monthly value**: *Total* (sum of all values in the month: leads, hours, revenue), *Average* (error rate, turnaround days) or *Latest* (rankings, MRR, a score).
- **Better when**: higher or lower. This decides whether a change shows as an improvement (green) or a decline (red).

## Archiving and deleting
**Archive** hides a client, stops reminders and frees a slot, keeping all data. **Delete** permanently removes the client and everything attached.
