# AI summaries (bring your own key)

ClientWrap can write report summaries, renewal suggestions, case studies and referral emails with AI. To keep lifetime plans sustainable and your costs transparent, you use **your own API key**:

1. Create a key at your provider (Anthropic Console or OpenAI Platform).
2. **Settings → Integrations → AI summaries**: choose the provider and model, paste the key, **Test & save**.

Keys are tested once, encrypted at rest and never shown again. Remove the key any time.

- Models only ever see the client name, service description and the numbers in that report.
- Without a key, ClientWrap's built-in writer creates factual summaries from your data. Every feature works without AI.
- Fair-use caps limit AI generations per month (see **Plan & usage**) to protect against runaway loops; usage is billed by your provider, not us.
