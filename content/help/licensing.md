# AppSumo licenses

## Redeem in 3 steps
1. On AppSumo, open your purchase and click **Activate**.
2. You arrive at ClientWrap: **create an account** (or **log in** if you already have one).
3. Done. Your tier is applied automatically and you're taken to the app.

You can also redeem manually: log in → **Plan & usage** → paste your license key → **Apply license**.

There's no rush: you can redeem any time after purchase (at least 60 days, and in practice as long as the license is valid).

Each tier includes everything in the tier below.

## Upgrades and downgrades
Change your tier on AppSumo. AppSumo notifies ClientWrap and your account switches automatically; your data stays. After a downgrade, if you have more active clients than the new limit allows, everything stays visible but you can't add clients until you archive some.

## License state comes from AppSumo only
We never edit licenses by hand: every change arrives from AppSumo. If something looks wrong, [contact support](/help/support) with your license key and we'll trace it.

See also [Refunds & deactivation](/help/refunds).
