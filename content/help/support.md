# Contact support

- **In the app:** open **Help & support** and send a message (we reply by email, usually within one business day). If chat is enabled you'll also see a chat bubble.
- **Email:** support@clientwrap.app

Please include your account email and, for licensing questions, your AppSumo license key. Every part of ClientWrap is self-serve; you never need to contact us to set anything up, but we're happy to help.
