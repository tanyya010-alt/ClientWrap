# Plans, limits & fair use

See [Pricing](/pricing) for plan details. **Plan & usage** in the app shows live usage meters.

| Monthly fair-use limit | Free | Solo | Growth | Agency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Active client workspaces | 1 | 5 | 15 | 40 |
| Emails sent | 50 | 1,000 | 3,000 | 8,000 |
| Webhook events | 500 | 20,000 | 60,000 | 150,000 |
| AI generations (your key) | 10 | 200 | 600 | 1,500 |
| SMS/WhatsApp (your Twilio) | — | — | 1,000 | 3,000 |

Limits reset on the 1st of each month (UTC). When you reach one, you get a clear message explaining what's paused, with an upgrade option; nothing is deleted, and anything skipped is listed in the Message log.
