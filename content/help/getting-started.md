# Getting started

ClientWrap gives each of your clients one private, branded link with their **monthly results**, **invoices** (with a pay button and friendly reminders) and a **suggested next step**. This guide gets you from sign-up to a sent report in about 10 minutes.

## 1. Create your account
Sign up with email and password or **Continue with Google**. Confirm your email address from the message we send you; this is required before ClientWrap can email your clients for you.

## 2. Follow the guided setup
Right after sign-up you land in the five-step setup:

1. **About you**: business name, what you sell (automation agency, SEO, coaching, design or other), timezone and currency. The service type pre-fills sensible metrics.
2. **Your brand**: pick a color and upload a logo (PNG/JPEG under 300 KB).
3. **First client**: name and contact. Tip: use your own email for the first test.
4. **Results**: type last month's and the previous month's numbers, or click *Use sample numbers*.
5. **First wrap**: ClientWrap builds the report, writes a summary and a next step, and you send it (to yourself first, if you like).

Just exploring? Click **Load demo workspace** on step 1 to get two sample clients with six months of data, invoices and a sent report. Demo clients never count toward your plan limits; remove them any time from the dashboard.

## 3. What to do next
- [Create an invoice](/help/invoices) with a Stripe pay button and automatic reminders.
- Automate results with a [CSV import](/help/csv-import) or a [webhook from Zapier, Make or n8n](/help/webhook).
- Turn on **scheduled delivery** in a client's settings so the wrap goes out every month by itself.
- Add your own [AI key](/help/ai-keys) for AI-written summaries (optional).

Stuck? [Contact support](/help/support): we answer every message.
