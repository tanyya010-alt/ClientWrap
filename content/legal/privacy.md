# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 September 2026_

This policy explains what personal data ClientWrap ("we") processes and why.

## Data about you (account holders), where we are the controller
- **Account data:** name, email, password hash or Google account ID, timezone.
- **Billing data:** plan, license keys, Stripe customer and subscription IDs (card details are handled by Stripe, never by us).
- **Usage and security data:** IP address, device/user agent, login times, audit log of actions, error logs.
- **Support messages** you send us.

We use this to provide and secure the Service, bill you, prevent abuse and answer support requests (legal bases: contract and legitimate interests). We do not sell personal data and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Data about your clients, where we are a processor
Contact names, emails, phone numbers, the business results you upload, invoices, messages and consent records are processed **on your behalf** under our [DPA](/legal/dpa).

## Sub-processors
| Provider | Purpose | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting provider (e.g. Vercel) | Application hosting | EU/US |
| Managed Postgres (e.g. Supabase) | Database & backups | EU/US |
| Resend | Email delivery | US |
| Stripe | Our subscription billing | US/EU |
| Sentry | Error monitoring | US/EU |

Services you connect yourself (your Stripe, Twilio, Anthropic/OpenAI accounts) receive data only when you use those features, under your own agreements with them.

## Retention
Account data is kept while your account exists. On account deletion, workspace data is deleted immediately and removed from backups within 30 days. After a license deactivation, client data is deleted 30 days later. Security logs are kept up to 12 months.

## Your rights
You can access, export, correct and delete your data in the app (Settings → Data & privacy), or email us. EU/UK residents may also object, restrict processing and complain to a supervisory authority.

## Security
Row-level security isolates every workspace; secrets are encrypted at rest; connections use TLS; access is logged.
