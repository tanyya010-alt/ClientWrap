# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

_Last updated: 24 September 2026_

This DPA forms part of the [Terms](/legal/terms) between you (the "Controller") and ClientWrap (the "Processor") and applies where we process personal data of your clients on your behalf, including under the GDPR and UK GDPR. It is accepted automatically when you accept the Terms; no signature is required.

## 1. Subject matter and duration
Processing of client personal data to provide the Service, for the duration of your account plus the deletion periods below.

## 2. Nature and purpose
Storage, display (client portal), report generation, invoicing, email/SMS/WhatsApp delivery and export, only on your documented instructions (your configuration and use of the Service).

## 3. Categories of data and data subjects
Your clients' contact persons: names, business emails, phone numbers, consent records, message history; business results data you upload.

## 4. Processor obligations
We will: process only on your instructions; ensure personnel are bound by confidentiality; implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (row-level tenant isolation, encryption of secrets at rest, TLS in transit, access logging, backups, least-privilege access); assist with data-subject requests (export and deletion tools are built in); notify you without undue delay (and within 72 hours where feasible) of a personal-data breach; delete or return data at the end of the service; and make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance.

## 5. Sub-processors
You authorise the sub-processors listed in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). We will give at least 14 days' notice of changes by email; you may object and terminate if we cannot reasonably accommodate the objection.

## 6. International transfers
Where data is transferred outside the EEA/UK, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum) with our sub-processors.

## 7. Deletion
On account deletion, data is deleted immediately from the live database and from backups within 30 days.
