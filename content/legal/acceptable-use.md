# Acceptable Use Policy

You may not use ClientWrap to:

- act as, or on behalf of, a debt collector or collection agency, or to collect debts owed to third parties;
- threaten, harass, shame or intimidate anyone, including with legal, credit-report or criminal threats;
- send messages to people who have not agreed to receive them, or ignore unsubscribe requests (we honour them automatically);
- send SMS or WhatsApp messages without recorded consent, or WhatsApp messages outside approved templates;
- upload unlawful content, malware, or data you have no right to process;
- probe, overload or attempt to bypass security or tenant isolation.

Our reminder templates and tone checks exist to keep messages friendly and professional. Accounts that breach this policy may be suspended.
