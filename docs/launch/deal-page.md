## Overview
Every month you do the same wrap-up for each client: pull the numbers, write an update, send an invoice, chase the invoice, and hope they renew. ClientWrap turns that into a 5-minute ritual.

**Results in your client's own units.** Hours saved, leads, rankings, goals completed. Type them, import any CSV (with column mapping and an error report), or send them from Zapier, Make or n8n with a signed webhook.

**The wrap writes itself.** ClientWrap builds a monthly report with headline numbers, change vs. last month and a clear summary of what changed and what's next (AI with your own key, or our built-in writer). Edit it, export a PDF, send it, or schedule it to go out automatically.

**Get paid without awkward chasing.** Invoices get a pay button on your own Stripe account. Reminders go out 3 days before and 1/7/14/30 days after the due date, in your client's timezone, outside quiet hours, with an unsubscribe link and tone guardrails. They stop automatically on payment.

**Grow every account.** A renewal suggestion appears right on the client's page. Draft a case study from real numbers that your client approves before it's published. Send a no-pressure referral request.

**Your brand.** Your logo and colors on everything. Tier 3 adds white-label, a custom domain and a custom email sender.
