# FAQ (deal page + site)

**Who is ClientWrap for?**
Solo consultants, freelancers and new agency owners who report results to clients monthly and invoice them: automation agencies, SEO, coaches, designers and any retainer-based service.

**Do my clients need to log in?**
No. Each client gets one private, signed link. It works on any device, and you can reset it anytime.

**Where does the results data come from?**
Manual entry, CSV import (any spreadsheet, with column mapping and an error report), or a signed, idempotent webhook with ready-made Zapier, Make and n8n snippets.

**Is the AI included?**
You bring your own Anthropic or OpenAI key, so you pay your provider directly and there's no markup and no credit system. Without a key, the built-in writer produces a factual summary from your numbers.

**How do payments work?**
Invoices get a Stripe Payment Link on your own Stripe account. Money goes straight to you. Payment status syncs automatically and reminders stop the moment the invoice is paid. No Stripe? Show bank-transfer instructions and mark invoices paid.

**Will reminders annoy my clients?**
They're designed not to: polite fixed templates, tone checks on custom text, quiet hours in the client's timezone, no weekends by default, one message per step, per-client pause, and an unsubscribe link in every message. ClientWrap is not a debt-collection tool.

**WhatsApp and SMS?**
On Tier 2+, with your own Twilio account, only for clients with a recorded consent, and WhatsApp only with approved templates. It's rolling out behind a feature flag.

**How do I redeem my AppSumo code?**
Click Activate on AppSumo → create an account or log in → done. Or paste your key under Plan & usage. You have at least 60 days to redeem.

**Can I upgrade or downgrade my tier?**
Yes, on AppSumo; it applies automatically and your data stays.

**What happens if I refund?**
Paid access stops, and your data stays read-only and exportable for 30 days, then it's deleted. You can redeem a new license 24 hours after a refund.

**What are the limits?**
Tier 1: 5 client workspaces · Tier 2: 15 · Tier 3: 40. Fair-use monthly caps on emails and webhook events are shown in the app with live meters.

**Is my clients' data safe?**
Row-level security on every table (with an automated cross-tenant test), encrypted secrets, rate limiting, signed links, an audit log, DPA available, export and delete anytime.

**What's not included?**
ClientWrap deliberately doesn't do CRM, lead generation, project management, team roles or native analytics integrations. Use the webhook to bring data from any tool.
