# From the founders

Hey Sumo-lings 👋

We built ClientWrap because we were tired of the monthly scramble.

Every client, every month: export numbers from three tools, paste them into a doc, try to explain why one number went down, attach an invoice, send it, and then quietly dread having to chase that invoice two weeks later. Renewals felt like luck. Case studies never got written. And referrals? We forgot to ask.

Talking to other freelancers and small agencies, we heard the same thing. **Clients don't read dashboards. They read a short note that says what happened, what's next, and how much they owe.** So that's what ClientWrap sends: one link per client, with results in *their* units, a clear summary and next step, and the invoice with a pay button right under it.

A few decisions we made on purpose:

- **Bring your own keys.** AI summaries, SMS and WhatsApp run on your own accounts. No credits, no markup, and it means a lifetime deal that stays sustainable for both of us. No key? The built-in writer still does the job.
- **Friendly by design.** Reminders respect quiet hours in your client's timezone, stop the moment they pay, include an unsubscribe link, and won't let anyone write threatening messages. Your client relationships are the asset; we protect them.
- **Your money goes to you.** Invoices use your own Stripe account. We never touch payments.
- **Security that's tested, not promised.** Every table is isolated with row-level security, and an automated test proves one workspace can't read another's data on every release.
- **Focused.** No CRM, no project management, no bloat. It's the tool you open once a month to wrap up each client in minutes.

We'd love your feedback in the Q&A: what metrics you report, which tools you want to pipe data from, and what would make your clients renew. Our public roadmap is shaped by what you tell us.

Thanks for backing us!

The ClientWrap team
